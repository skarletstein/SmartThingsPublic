# Can You Trust AI?: shot list
60 s · 120 bpm · 120 beats (0.5 s each) · format: 9x16 (1080×1920)
Music: synth house, D minor, drop @ beat 16 · VO: ElevenLabs, `audio/script.txt`

## Look
Near-black `#0A0A0B` + bone white `#EDEBE6`, ONE accent: signal acid `#C8FF2E`.
Display: Inter Display Black (huge, tight tracking). UI: JetBrains Mono Bold (machine voice: labels, the
"terminal" lines). Recurring object: **THE TRUST METER**, a giant half-dial with a needle that the
whole film argues over. Glitch slicing + RGB split only in the AGAINST section (it earns it there).

## Script (≈110 words, ~2.4 w/s)
```
You already trust AI. Every single day.
Your feed. Your maps. Your bank.
So... should you?
The case FOR.
It helps doctors catch disease earlier.
It flags fraud in milliseconds.
It translates, tutors, and never sleeps.
The case AGAINST.
It makes things up. Confidently.
It copies our biases.
It can fake a voice. A face. A whole video.
And it's built by companies chasing your attention.
So, the verdict?
Don't trust it blindly. Don't fear it either.
Treat it like a brilliant intern: fast, tireless, and you check its work.
Ask who built it, and what happens when it's wrong.
Trust. But verify.
```
